Contact

Found a bug, spotted a wrong result, or want to suggest a calculator? We'd like to hear about it.

Email us

The fastest way to reach us is email: hello@calcbundle.net

What's useful to include

Reporting a wrong or unexpected result?

Let us know which calculator, the exact numbers you entered, and what result you expected versus what you got — that's usually enough for us to track down the issue.

Suggesting a new calculator?

Tell us what it should calculate and, if you have one in mind, an example of the inputs and outputs you'd expect to see.

Something else?

General feedback, partnership questions, or anything else — just send it our way.

CalcBundle is a small, independently run site with no live chat or contact form — email is the only channel we check, and we read everything even if a reply takes a little while.
