Density Calculator

Solve for density, volume, or mass using ρ = m / V, given the other two values.

The density equation

Density (ρ) is mass per unit volume: ρ = m / V. Rearranged, volume = m / ρ, and mass = ρ × V. Pick which quantity you want to solve for, enter the other two, and this calculator handles the unit conversions internally.

Why units matter so much here

Density values look very different depending on the units used — water is "1" in g/cm³ but "1000" in kg/m³ and about "62.4" in lb/ft³. All three describe the same density; only the units differ. Always double check the unit next to your result, not just the number.

Density can change with temperature and pressure, especially for gases — the value you enter should reflect the conditions relevant to your calculation.

A worked example

A block with a mass of 50 pounds and a volume of 2 cubic feet has a density of about 400.46 kg/m³. The page converts the units for you: 50 pounds is 22.68 kilograms and 2 cubic feet is 0.0566 cubic meters, and 22.68 ÷ 0.0566 gives 400.46. For comparison, water is 1,000 kg/m³, so this material would float.

Tips for density

Common questions

What's the density of water, for reference?

About 1,000 kg/m³ (1 g/cm³, or 62.4 lb/ft³) at 4°C, which is why those numbers show up so often as a sanity check.

Can I use this for gases as well as liquids and solids?

Yes — the equation is the same for any material, though gas densities vary much more with temperature and pressure than liquids or solids.
