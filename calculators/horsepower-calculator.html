What the calculator does

The page has two tools. The power from force, distance and time tool works out the rate of doing work and shows it in watts, mechanical horsepower, metric horsepower and kilowatts. The power converter changes an amount between watts, kilowatts, four kinds of horsepower and BTU per hour. Power is the rate of doing work: power = force × distance ÷ time.

Worked example with the default numbers

The first tool opens on a force of 10 newtons moving an object 3 metres in 2 seconds. The work is 10 × 3 = 30 joules, and the power is 30 ÷ 2 = 15 watts. In other units that is 0.020115 mechanical horsepower, 0.020394 metric horsepower and 0.015 kilowatts. The converter opens on 1 mechanical horsepower converted to watts, which is 745.7 W.

Mechanical (imperial) horsepower is the common US and UK unit. Metric horsepower, written PS or CV, is used by many car makers in Europe. Electrical horsepower is exactly 746 W and is used for motors, and boiler horsepower measures the heat output of a boiler. Converting a car rating

A car rated at 150 mechanical horsepower has 111,855 watts of power, which is 111.9 kW, or 152.1 PS. Going the other way, 100 PS is 98.6 mechanical horsepower. The difference is about 1.4%, small but worth knowing when two catalogues use different units.

Lifting a load

Raising a 70 kg-force load (the weight of a 70 kg person) by 3 metres in 5 seconds needs a force of 70 × 9.80665 = 686.5 N, so the power is 686.5 × 3 ÷ 5 = 411.9 W, about 0.552 mechanical horsepower. Moving a 500 lbf load 10 yards in a minute takes 339 W. Choose the force unit, newton, kilonewton, pound-force or kilogram-force, and the tool does the conversion.

Motors and boilers

A 5 horsepower electric motor delivers 3,728 W at the shaft. If it is 90% efficient it draws 4,143 W, or 4.14 kW, from the supply. Running for an hour at $0.16 per kWh costs about $0.66. Use the Electricity Calculator with your own rate. A boiler horsepower is 9,809.5 W, which the converter shows as about 33,471 BTU/h.

Horsepower from torque and speed

This calculator starts from force, distance and time. For an engine you often know torque and rotational speed. In US units, horsepower = torque (lb-ft) × rpm ÷ 5,252, where 5,252 is 33,000 ÷ 2π. An engine making 300 lb-ft at 4,000 rpm produces 228.5 horsepower. At 5,252 rpm, torque in lb-ft and horsepower are equal. The Engine Horsepower Calculator handles torque and rpm.

Common mistakes

  • Treating all horsepower units as equal. Metric and mechanical differ by about 1.4%.
  • Mixing units, such as a force in pounds-force with a distance in metres, without choosing the unit in the box.
  • Confusing power with energy. Power is a rate, so 15 W for 2 seconds is 30 joules.
  • Using torque alone. Horsepower also needs speed.
  • Forgetting that motor input is higher than output because of losses.

How to read your result

The large number is the power in watts, with the same value in mechanical horsepower, metric horsepower and kilowatts underneath. In the converter, the large number is your amount in the unit you chose. Values are shown to up to six decimal places.

These are unit conversions and a basic physics formula. Real engines and motors lose some power to friction, heat and other losses.

Common questions

How is power calculated?

Power is force times distance divided by time. A force of 10 newtons moving an object 3 metres in 2 seconds does 30 joules of work in 2 seconds, which is 15 watts.

How many watts is one horsepower?

One mechanical horsepower is 745.7 watts. Metric horsepower is about 735.5 watts, electrical horsepower is 746 watts and boiler horsepower is 9,809.5 watts.

What is the difference between hp and PS?

PS is metric horsepower, about 735.5 watts, while hp is usually mechanical horsepower at 745.7 watts. 100 PS is about 98.6 hp.

How do I get horsepower from torque?

Multiply torque in pound-feet by rpm and divide by 5,252. That formula is for engines, and the Engine Horsepower Calculator does it for you.

Why do different car sites show different horsepower?

They may use different units, such as metric PS or kilowatts, or measure at the engine or at the wheels.

What is boiler horsepower?

It is a unit for the heat output of boilers, equal to 9,809.5 watts, which is about 33,470 BTU per hour.
