Quick answer. TDEE is the calories you burn in a day. A 25-year-old man who is 5 ft 10 in and 165 lb has a BMR of 1,740 and, with moderate exercise, a TDEE of 2,549 calories a day. Eating about 500 fewer calories is the usual target for losing 1 pound a week.

What TDEE means

Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) is everything your body burns in a day: your basal metabolic rate (energy used at rest), the energy to digest food, and all movement, from daily activity to deliberate exercise. This calculator estimates BMR with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and multiplies it by an activity factor. The result is also your maintenance level: eating that many calories should keep your weight steady.

The formula

  • BMR (men) = 10 × weight in kg + 6.25 × height in cm − 5 × age + 5.
  • BMR (women) = 10 × weight in kg + 6.25 × height in cm − 5 × age − 161.
  • TDEE = BMR × activity factor.

Worked example with the default numbers

A 25-year-old man who is 5 ft 10 in (177.8 cm) and weighs 165 lb (74.8 kg). His BMR is 1,740 calories a day. With moderate exercise four to five times a week the factor is 1.465, so his TDEE is 2,549 calories a day. His BMI is 23.7, in the normal range. A woman of the same age, height and weight would have a BMR of 1,574 and a TDEE of 2,305, because the formula subtracts 161 instead of adding 5.

Pick the level that matches an ordinary week, not your best week. Light is exercise one to three times a week, moderate four to five times, active daily or hard exercise three to four times, and the top two levels are for very intense training or a physical job.

Using TDEE for a goal

Eating at your TDEE maintains weight. A deficit leads to loss and a surplus to gain. The calculator uses the rule that about 3,500 calories equals 1 pound of body weight, so 500 calories a day is about 1 pound a week. The table shows each target and its share of TDEE:

The 3,500-calorie rule is a simple estimate. As you lose weight your TDEE falls, so the same deficit produces slower loss over time, and the NIDDK Body Weight Planner models this. Cutting more than about 1,000 calories a day below TDEE is generally not recommended, since it risks muscle loss; do not eat below your BMR without medical supervision.

How age changes it

What the estimate misses

  • Muscle mass: muscle burns more than fat, so two people of the same weight can differ. The Calorie Calculator offers the Katch-McArdle formula if you know your body fat.
  • Daily variation: NEAT (fidgeting, walking, standing) can differ a lot between people.
  • Medical conditions, medicines, pregnancy and breastfeeding.
  • Measurement error in your own weight and activity.

Turning the number into a plan

Treat the TDEE as a starting point. Track your weight for two to three weeks, using a weekly average, and adjust by 100 to 200 calories if it does not move as expected. Aim for no more than 1 to 2 pounds of loss a week. For the split of protein, carbohydrate and fat, use the Macro Calculator.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing a higher activity level than your week really has.
  • Treating TDEE as exact. It is an estimate within roughly a few hundred calories.
  • Cutting too deeply and too fast.
  • Counting exercise calories twice, once in the activity factor and again as extra food allowance.

How to read your result

The large number is your maintenance calories. Below it are your BMI, your BMR and the calorie targets for gaining and losing weight. If your weight does not respond as the table predicts after a few weeks, adjust the calories rather than trusting the formula.

This calculator gives estimates for healthy adults, not medical advice. Ask a doctor or dietitian about your own needs.

Common questions

How is TDEE different from BMR?

BMR is the energy your body needs at complete rest. TDEE adds everything else, activity, exercise and digestion, on top of BMR, so it is always a higher number.

How is this different from the Calorie Calculator?

They compute the same underlying number, but the Calorie Calculator also lets you pick between three BMR formulas, including one based on body fat percentage, if you want more control over the estimate.

Which activity level should I choose?

Choose what matches an ordinary week. Most people overrate their activity, so if you are unsure, choose the lower level and adjust after tracking your weight.

Is the 3,500 calories per pound rule exact?

No. It is a rough estimate. As you lose weight your energy needs fall, so loss usually slows over time at the same intake.

How many calories below TDEE is safe?

A deficit of 250 to 500 calories a day is the usual range. Going more than about 1,000 below TDEE is generally not recommended without medical supervision.

Why is the result different for men and women?

The Mifflin-St Jeor formula adds 5 for men and subtracts 161 for women, reflecting average differences in body composition.
