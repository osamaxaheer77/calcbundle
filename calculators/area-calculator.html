Area Calculator

Select the shape before entering its dimensions. Different shapes require different measurements, and the area result uses square units rather than linear units.

By Osama · Last reviewed November 6, 2026

Quick answer. Area is the space inside a shape, in square units. The default 220 by 99 rectangle is 21,780 square feet, exactly half an acre. A circle of radius 15 has an area of 706.85835, and a triangle with sides of 3, 4 and 5 has an area of 6.

What the calculator does

Choose a shape, enter its measurements and the calculator gives the area. It covers seven shapes: rectangle, triangle, trapezoid, circle, sector, ellipse and parallelogram. The answer is in square units: if your lengths are in feet, the area is in square feet, and if they are in metres, it is in square metres. Use the same unit for every length in one calculation.

Worked example with the default numbers

The page opens on a rectangle of 220 by 99. Area = length × width = 220 × 99 = 21,780. If the lengths are feet, that is 21,780 square feet, which is exactly half an acre because an acre is 43,560 square feet. It is also about 2,023.42821 square metres. If both measurements double, the area becomes four times as large, not twice, since both length and width are multiplied.

Rectangle

Multiply length by width. A 12 by 10 room has 120 square feet, and a 6 by 4 closet has 24. Rectangles are the shape for floors, walls, lots and sheets of material. For a room, the Square Footage Calculator handles several rectangles and a wall or floor material estimate.

Triangle

The triangle tool uses Heron’s formula, so you only need the three side lengths and no angle. The defaults, 77, 77 and 77, form an equilateral triangle. The semiperimeter is s = 115.5, so the area is √[115.5 × 38.5 × 38.5 × 38.5] = 2,567.33231. You can check it with half base times height: the height is 77 × √3 ÷ 2 = 66.68396, and ½ × 77 × 66.68396 gives the same area. For a 3-4-5 triangle the area is 6. If the three sides cannot form a triangle, because two of them add up to no more than the third, the calculator says so.

Trapezoid

Average the two parallel bases and multiply by the height. With bases 29.528 and 9 and a height of 9, the average base is 19.264 and the area is 19.264 × 9 = 173.376. The height is the straight distance between the parallel sides, not the length of the slanted side.

Circle

Area is π times the radius squared. With a radius of 15, the area is π × 225 = 706.85835. Enter the radius, not the diameter; half the diameter is the radius. Because the radius is squared, a circle twice as wide has four times the area.

Sector

A sector is a slice of a circle, so its area is the circle’s area times the fraction of the circle it covers. With a radius of 16 and an angle of 60°, the fraction is 60 ÷ 360 = 1/6 and the area is 1/6 × π × 256 = 134.04129. You can enter the angle in radians instead: 1.0472 radians is the same 60°, and 1 radian on a radius of 16 gives 128.

Ellipse

An ellipse is a circle stretched along two radii. The area is π × a × b. The defaults are a = 18 and b = 20, which give 1,130.97336. The calculator does not need the first axis to be the larger one, since the formula treats both the same. If a and b are equal, the shape is a circle: a = b = 19 gives 1,134.11495.

Parallelogram

Multiply the base by the perpendicular height. With a base of 20 and a height of 18, the area is 360, the same as a 20 by 18 rectangle. The slanted side does not matter, only the base and the height measured at right angles to it.

Breaking down complex shapes

Most irregular shapes can be split into the simple ones above. Calculate each piece, then add them. An L-shaped room made of a 12 by 10 rectangle and a 6 by 4 rectangle has 120 + 24 = 144 square feet. To find a ring, such as a circular path around a pond, subtract the inner circle from the outer: with radii 15 and 10 the ring is π × (225 − 100) = 392.69908. For a shape with angles or sides to find, use the Triangle Calculator.

Units and conversions

The calculator does not convert units, so convert the lengths first or convert the answer afterwards. Area units are squared, so the factors are squared too: 1 foot is 0.3048 metres, so 1 square foot is 0.3048² = 0.09290304 square metres.

Getting a sense of scale

The default rectangle, 21,780 square feet, is half an acre. It helps to compare it with places you know.

Everyday problems

  • Painting a wall. A wall 12 feet wide and 8 feet high is 96 square feet. Take off a door of 3 by 7 (21) and a window of 3 by 4 (12) and you need to paint 63 square feet.
  • Flooring. A 12 by 10 room is 120 square feet. Many installers suggest adding about 5% to 10% for cuts and waste, so a 10% allowance means buying 132 square feet.
  • Round tablecloth. A table with a radius of 24 inches has a top of π × 576 = 1,809.55737 square inches, or 12.57 square feet.
  • Two pizzas. A 12-inch pizza (radius 6) has 113.09734 square inches and a 16-inch pizza (radius 8) has 201.06193, which is 78% more pizza for a diameter only one third larger.

Why the formulas work

The rectangle formula counts unit squares: 220 columns of 99 squares. A parallelogram has the same area as a rectangle with the same base and height, because the slanted end can be cut off and moved to the other side. A triangle is half of a parallelogram, and a trapezoid is two triangles that share its height, which is where the average of the two bases comes from. A circle can be cut into thin sectors and rearranged into a rough parallelogram of base πr and height r, giving πr². An ellipse is a circle stretched by a factor a in one direction and b in the other, so its area is that of a unit circle times a times b.

Area versus perimeter

Area measures the space inside a shape, in square units. Perimeter measures the distance around it, in plain units. They answer different questions: area tells you how much paint or flooring you need, perimeter tells you how much fencing or edging. For the default 220 by 99 rectangle the perimeter is 2 × (220 + 99) = 638, while the circumference of the radius-15 circle is 2 × π × 15 = 94.24778. Two shapes can have the same area and very different perimeters, so check which one the job needs.

Common mistakes

  • Mixing units, such as feet for the length and inches for the width.
  • Reporting the area in feet instead of square feet.
  • Using the diameter in the circle box instead of the radius.
  • Using the slanted side as the height of a trapezoid or parallelogram.
  • Entering an angle in degrees when the box is set to radians, or the other way round.
  • Expecting double the sides to give double the area. It gives four times the area.

How to read your result

The number shown is the area in square units, with up to five decimal places. Write the unit yourself: square feet, square metres, and so on. For a quote, add an allowance for waste and cuts, since material is bought in whole pieces.

The results are exact for the numbers you enter. Measure carefully, and check the shape matches the real object before you buy materials.

Common questions

How do I find the area of a rectangle?

Multiply the length by the width. A 220 by 99 rectangle has an area of 21,780, which is half an acre if the lengths are in feet.

How is the area of a triangle calculated here?

The calculator uses Heron's formula, which needs only the three side lengths. It finds the semiperimeter and then takes the square root of s times (s minus a) times (s minus b) times (s minus c).

Do I enter the radius or the diameter for a circle?

Enter the radius, which is half the diameter. The area is pi times the radius squared.

Why does the area change by a factor of four when I double the sides?

Area is measured in square units, so doubling both dimensions multiplies the area by two times two, which is four.

What is the difference between a sector and a circle?

A sector is a slice of a circle. Its area is the circle's area times the fraction of the full 360 degrees that the slice covers.

How many square feet are in an acre?

An acre is 43,560 square feet, which is about 4,047 square metres.
