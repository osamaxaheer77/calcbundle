What a ratio is

A ratio compares two quantities and shows how many times one contains the other. A ratio of 3 to 4, written 3 : 4, means that for every 3 of the first thing there are 4 of the second. Ratios appear in recipes, maps, screen sizes, mixing paint and sharing money. The same ratio can be written many ways, and 3 : 4, 6 : 8 and 600 : 800 all describe the same relationship.

Solving a proportion

A proportion says two ratios are equal, A : B = C : D, or as fractions A ÷ B = C ÷ D. If you know any three of the values, multiply across to find the fourth. D equals B × C ÷ A, and C equals A × D ÷ B. A equals B × C ÷ D, and B equals A × D ÷ C. For 3 : 4 = 600 : D, D is 4 × 600 ÷ 3, which is 800. None of A, B, C or D can be zero in the calculation.

Simplest form and scaling

To simplify a ratio of whole numbers, divide both numbers by their greatest common factor. The calculator also writes the ratio as 1 : x and x : 1, which is useful for comparing, and shows what share of the total each part makes up. The scaling calculator multiplies or divides both sides of a ratio by the same number, so enlarging 250 : 280 by 2.5 times gives 625 : 700.

Ratios keep their shape when both sides are multiplied or divided by the same number. Results are rounded to about 10 decimal places.

Common questions

How do you solve a ratio problem?

Write it as a proportion, A : B = C : D, and cross-multiply. The missing value is the product of the two numbers on the diagonal that are known, divided by the number opposite the unknown.

How do you simplify a ratio?

Divide both numbers by their greatest common factor. For example, 600 : 800 divided by 200 is 3 : 4.

What does a ratio of 1 : 1.33 mean?

It means the second quantity is 1.33 times the first. It is the same relationship as 3 : 4, written with 1 on the left.

How do I find what percentage a ratio part is?

Divide the part by the total of both parts. For 3 : 4 the first part is 3 ÷ 7, which is about 42.9%.

How do I resize an image in the same ratio?

Use the scaling calculator. Enter the width and height, choose enlarge or shrink, and enter how many times, and it gives the new width and height in the same ratio.
