Healthy Weight Calculator

Enter your height to see the healthy weight range for it, and the weights that fall in each BMI category.

By Osama · Last reviewed November 6, 2026

Quick answer. A healthy weight for adults is the weight that gives a BMI from 18.5 up to 25, which is BMI × height squared. At 180 cm (5 ft 11 in) the healthy range is 59.9 to 81.0 kg (132 to 179 lb).

How the healthy weight range is found

The range comes from body mass index (BMI), which is weight divided by height squared. For adults, a BMI from 18.5 up to (but not including) 25 is described as a healthy weight. To turn that into weights for your height, the calculator multiplies your height in metres squared by 18.5 for the lowest healthy weight and by 25 for the highest: weight = BMI × height².

Worked example with the default height

Height 180 cm = 1.80 m, so height² = 3.24. The lowest healthy weight is 18.5 × 3.24 = 59.9 kg (132 lb) and the highest is 25 × 3.24 = 81.0 kg (179 lb).

What the range cannot tell you

  • BMI uses only height and weight. It cannot separate muscle, bone and fat, so a muscular person can sit in the overweight range and still be healthy.
  • It does not show where fat is carried. Fat around the waist carries more health risk than fat on the hips.
  • It does not consider age, fitness or family history.
  • It is meant for adults. Children and teens need growth charts for their age and sex.
  • Some health organisations suggest lower BMI cut-offs for people of Asian descent, and some experts accept a slightly higher BMI in older adults. Ask a doctor what applies to you.

Using the range sensibly

A range is more useful than a single target. If you are inside it, a few pounds either way matter little. If you are outside it, look at other signs of health such as waist size, blood pressure, blood sugar and how you feel. The Body Fat Calculator and the BMI vs body fat guide help separate fat from muscle.

Common mistakes

  • Entering height in inches in the centimetres box, or the reverse. Check the unit selector.
  • Aiming for the bottom of the range. Being healthy does not mean being at the lowest weight in the range.
  • Treating the range as a medical target.
  • Using it for children.

How to read your result

Check where your current weight sits among the bands. If you are within the healthy range, focus on habits rather than the number. If you are above or below, plan changes gradually and speak to a doctor or dietitian, especially if you have a health condition.

This calculator gives a general guide for adults and is not a medical diagnosis. Speak to a doctor or registered dietitian for advice about your weight.

Common questions

What is a healthy weight for my height?

It is the weight that gives a BMI from 18.5 to just under 25. Enter your height and the calculator shows that range in kilograms or pounds.

How is the healthy range calculated?

Square your height in metres and multiply by 18.5 for the lowest healthy weight and by 25 for the highest.

Is the BMI range the same for men and women?

The standard adult ranges are the same for both. Body composition differs between men and women, which is why BMI is only a screening guide.

Can I be healthy outside the range?

Yes. Muscular people may be above it while healthy, and some people inside the range have too much body fat. Waist size, fitness and medical tests give a fuller picture.

Does age change the healthy range?

The calculator uses the standard adult ranges. Some experts accept a slightly higher BMI in older adults, so ask a doctor if you are unsure.

Why is the range wider for taller people?

BMI uses height squared, so the same BMI band covers more kilograms as height increases.
