Quick answer. January 1 to March 1, 2026 is 59 days: 17 weekend days, 3 holidays and 39 business days. Five business days after a Monday is the next Monday, and 15 business days after November 16, 2026 is Tue, Dec 8, 2026.

Two tools in one page

The page has two calculators. The first, days between two dates, counts the days from a start date to an end date and splits them into weekend days, holidays and business days. The second, date offset, moves a date forward or back by a number of calendar days or business days. Both use the same holiday list, which you can change in the settings box.

The date boxes open with today’s date, plus 30 days for the end date and 15 days for the offset, so the numbers below use fixed 2026 dates instead. You can enter the same dates to reproduce them.

How the days are counted

The count runs from the start date up to, but not including, the end date, so Monday to the next Monday is 7 days. Tick “Include the end day” to add 1, which suits a hotel stay or a project length where both the first and last day count. If the start is after the end, the two dates are swapped. A leap-year February is counted correctly, and the tool accepts dates from year 1 to 9999.

Worked example: January 1 to March 1, 2026

January has 31 days and February 2026 has 28, so January 1 to March 1 is 59 days. Of those, 17 fall on a Saturday or Sunday and 42 are weekdays. Three default holidays land in the range: New Year’s Day (Thursday, January 1), Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Monday, January 19) and Presidents’ Day (Monday, February 16). All three are weekdays, so the business-day count is 42 − 3 = 39.

Worked example: counting both end days

A project runs from Monday, June 1 to Friday, July 31, 2026, with the end day included. The count becomes 61 days (June has 30 and July has 31). Two holidays fall in the range: Juneteenth on Friday, June 19 and Independence Day on Saturday, July 4. The tool lists both under Holidays (2), but July 4 is already a weekend, so only Juneteenth lowers the business days: 45 weekdays minus 1 gives 44 business days, with 16 weekend days.

Business days and holidays

A business day is a weekday, Monday to Friday, that is not a chosen holiday. The list starts with these eleven U.S. federal holidays ticked: New Year’s Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents’ Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas Day. Black Friday, Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve are available but unticked, and you can add up to six holidays of your own with a month and day. Here is how the defaults fall in 2026:

The tool places each holiday on its calendar date, or on its usual weekday rule such as the third Monday of January. It does not move a holiday that falls on a weekend to the nearest weekday, as the federal government does, so July 4, 2026 stays on Saturday. Add your own date if your business observes a different day.

Finding a date: days after or before

Choose a start date, a direction and a number of days from 1 to 100,000. With the business-days box off the tool just adds or subtracts calendar days: 30 days after Monday, December 1, 2026 is Thu, Dec 31, 2026. With the box on, it steps over weekends and the chosen holidays, and the start day is not counted. So 5 business days after Monday, March 2, 2026 is Mon, Mar 9, 2026, the next Monday.

For a longer span, 15 business days after Monday, November 16, 2026 lands on Tue, Dec 8, 2026. That is 22 calendar days later, one more than the 21 it would take with no holidays, because Thanksgiving on Thursday, November 26 is skipped. This follows the same logic as the working-day functions in spreadsheet programs.

What the result shows

  • Days between: the total, with the start and end days named, plus weekend days.
  • Holidays and business days: shown when holidays are turned on, with each holiday listed with its weekday. With holidays off, the tool shows weekdays instead.
  • Calendars: one small calendar for each month in the range, up to 12 months, shading weekends and holidays and outlining the dates you chose.
  • Date offset: the date reached, the start date, the days in between and, for business days, the weekends and holidays skipped.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting that the end day is not counted unless the box is ticked.
  • Assuming a holiday on a weekend reduces the business days. It does not, because the weekend is already off.
  • Expecting observed holidays. A Saturday holiday is not moved to Friday here.
  • Using calendar days where a contract says business days, or the reverse. Check how your agreement counts days.
  • Leaving a holiday ticked that your organisation does not observe.

How to read your result

The large number is the days between the two dates, or the date reached when you offset. Business days appear below it. If a deadline depends on the answer, compare it with the exact wording in your contract, since some agreements count the start day, skip other holidays or end at a set time.

The calculator treats Saturday and Sunday as the weekend and uses the holiday dates for the year they fall in. It is a planning aid, not legal advice about deadlines.

Common questions

How do I count the days between two dates?

Subtract the earlier date from the later one. This calculator does it for you and can add 1 to include the end day. January 1 to March 1, 2026 is 59 days.

How are business days counted?

They are the weekdays, Monday to Friday, in the period, minus any holidays that fall on those weekdays. A holiday on a weekend does not lower the count.

Which holidays are included?

The common U.S. federal holidays are on by default, and you can switch each one off or add Black Friday, Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve. You can also enter six holidays of your own.

What does “5 business days after” mean?

It means moving forward five weekdays that are not holidays, not counting the start day. Five business days after a Monday is the next Monday.

Why does the count not include the end day?

Counting from one date up to another gives the number of days passed. Tick the include box if you want both the first and last day counted, as for a hotel stay or a project length.

Does the tool move holidays that fall on a weekend?

No. A holiday on a Saturday or Sunday stays on that date and does not change the business-day count. Add your own date if you need the observed day.
