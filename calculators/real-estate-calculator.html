Real Estate Calculator

"Real estate" covers a lot of ground — buying, financing, refinancing, renting, and investing all have their own math. Rather than one do-everything tool, here's a directory of every CalcBundle calculator built for a specific real estate question.

Which calculator to use at each step

Before you look at homes. Start with the House Affordability Calculator to see what price fits your income and debts, and check your ratios with the Debt Ratio Calculator, since lenders look closely at them. The Down Payment Calculator shows how much to save, and how a bigger down payment changes your loan.

While you compare loans. The Mortgage Calculator gives the monthly payment with taxes and insurance. If you may qualify for a government-backed loan, compare it with the FHA Loan Calculator or the VA Mortgage Calculator. Use the APR Calculator to compare offers with different fees, and the Amortization Calculator to see how each payment splits between principal and interest.

After you buy. The Mortgage Payoff Calculator shows what extra payments save, and the Refinance Calculator shows whether a lower rate pays for its costs and when you break even. If you need to borrow against your home, compare the Home Equity Loan Calculator and the HELOC Calculator.

Renting or investing. The Rent vs. Buy Calculator compares the long-term cost of each choice, and the Rent Calculator helps you decide how much rent you can afford. For an investment property, the Rental Property Calculator estimates cash flow and return.

A few things to remember

Every home purchase has costs beyond the mortgage payment: property tax, insurance, maintenance, closing costs and, with a small down payment, mortgage insurance. Build them into your budget before you commit. All calculations on CalcBundle run in your browser and give estimates, so confirm the figures with a lender before you rely on them.
