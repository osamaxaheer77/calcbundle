Dice Roller

Roll virtual dice online with any number of sides and any number of dice, and see each roll and the total, using a secure random number generator.

How the rolls are generated

Each roll uses your browser's cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues), the same source used by our Password Generator — so results are genuinely unpredictable, not just a simple pseudo-random sequence.

Beyond standard six-sided dice

Set "Sides Per Die" to whatever you need — 4, 8, 10, 12, and 20 are common for tabletop games, but any number of sides works. "Number of Dice" lets you roll several at once and see both the total and each individual result.

Every roll happens locally in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere, and each click generates a fresh, independent result.

A worked example

Roll two six-sided dice and the total is between 2 and 12. The most likely total is 7, which comes up in 6 of the 36 possible outcomes, or one roll in six, while 2 and 12 each come up only once in 36. The results show the total, each individual die and the average, so you can see how the rolls behaved.

Tips for tabletop games

Set the sides to match the die your game calls for: 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 20 are the usual set. To roll with advantage in a game that uses a twenty-sided die, roll two of them and take the higher. Because each click is an independent roll, a long streak of the same number is normal, and it does not mean the next roll is more or less likely.

Common uses

People use a virtual dice roller for board games when the dice are missing, for role-playing games, for classroom probability lessons, and for making quick random choices between options.

Common questions

Can I roll dice with an odd number of sides, like a d5 or d7?

Yes — any whole number of sides 2 or greater works, even non-standard ones.

What's the maximum number of dice I can roll at once?

Up to 100 dice in a single roll.
