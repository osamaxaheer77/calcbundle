Dice Roller

Roll virtual dice with a customizable number of sides and dice count.

How the rolls are generated

Each roll uses your browser's cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues), the same source used by our Password Generator — so results are genuinely unpredictable, not just a simple pseudo-random sequence.

Beyond standard six-sided dice

Set "Sides Per Die" to whatever you need — 4, 8, 10, 12, and 20 are common for tabletop games, but any number of sides works. "Number of Dice" lets you roll several at once and see both the total and each individual result.

Every roll happens locally in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere, and each click generates a fresh, independent result.

Common questions

Can I roll dice with an odd number of sides, like a d5 or d7?

Yes — any whole number of sides 2 or greater works, even non-standard ones.

What's the maximum number of dice I can roll at once?

Up to 100 dice in a single roll.
