Body Type Calculator

Enter four body measurements to find your body shape for clothing ideas, and see your waist-hip ratio.

Stand straight with your arms at your sides. Keep the tape snug against the body without squeezing.

By Osama · Last reviewed November 6, 2026

Quick answer. Body shape is worked out from four measurements: bust, waist, high hip and hip. Bust 90, waist 60, high hip 80 and hip 90 cm gives an hourglass shape with a waist-hip ratio of 0.67; the WHO treats a ratio above 0.85 in women as higher risk.

How to measure

  • Bust: around the fullest part of the chest, wearing a well-fitting bra.
  • Waist: the smallest circumference of the natural waist, just above the belly button.
  • High hip: the upper swell of the hip over the pelvis, about 7 inches (18 cm) below the natural waist.
  • Hip: the widest circumference around the hips and buttocks.

Stand upright with your arms relaxed, and keep the tape level and snug but not tight. You can enter each measurement in inches or centimetres, and the units can differ between boxes.

The seven body shapes

The calculator uses rules from a published study of body shapes in American and Korean women (Lee, Istook, Nam and Park, 2007) that sorted bodies into types such as hourglass, bottom hourglass, top hourglass, spoon, triangle, inverted triangle and rectangle. Each type is defined by the differences between bust, waist and hips and by the ratio of the high hip to the waist. The rules, in inches:

ShapeRule used by the calculator
HourglassBust no more than 1 inch bigger than the hips, hips less than 3.6 inches bigger than the bust, and a waist at least 9 inches smaller than the bust or 10 inches smaller than the hips
Bottom hourglassHips 3.6 to 10 inches bigger than the bust, hips at least 9 inches bigger than the waist, high hip to waist ratio under 1.193
Top hourglassBust 1 to 10 inches bigger than the hips and at least 9 inches bigger than the waist
SpoonHips more than 2 inches bigger than the bust and at least 7 inches bigger than the waist, high hip to waist ratio of 1.193 or more
TriangleHips at least 3.6 inches bigger than the bust, but less than 9 inches bigger than the waist
Inverted triangleBust at least 3.6 inches bigger than the hips, but less than 9 inches bigger than the waist
RectangleBust and hips within 3.6 inches of each other, waist within 9 inches of the bust and 10 inches of the hips

Worked example with the default numbers

Bust 90 cm, waist 60 cm, high hip 80 cm and hip 90 cm (35.4, 23.6, 31.5 and 35.4 inches). The bust and hips are equal, and the waist is 11.8 inches smaller than both, which meets the hourglass rule, so the calculator says Hourglass. The waist-hip ratio is 60 ÷ 90 = 0.67.

Why you can get two shapes, or none

The rules overlap, so some measurements match more than one shape. For bust 92, waist 70, high hip 84 and hip 98 cm the calculator lists Hourglass and Spoon. Other measurements fit none of the seven shapes, in which case the calculator says it could not place them. That does not mean anything is wrong: the rules cannot cover every body.

Waist-hip ratio

The waist-hip ratio (WHR) is your waist divided by your hip measurement. It is a simple way to see where your body carries weight. The World Health Organization treats a WHR above 0.85 in women as indicating abdominal obesity and a higher health risk, and the calculator adds a note when your ratio is above 0.85. Researchers have found WHR a better predictor of heart disease than weight alone. For men, the WHO cut-off is 0.90, but this calculator is built for women’s body shapes.

How to use the result

  • Use your shape for choosing clothes that fit well: it describes proportions, not health.
  • Body shape categories are descriptive and vary between cultures.
  • A waist-hip ratio is one screening number, not a diagnosis. For body composition, see the Body Fat Calculator; for weight against height, the BMI Calculator.
  • If you are worried about your health, speak to a doctor.

Common mistakes

  • Measuring over thick clothing or pulling the tape too tight.
  • Measuring the waist at the narrowest point of the torso instead of the natural waist, or the hip at the wrong height.
  • Swapping the high hip and hip boxes.
  • Treating a shape as a goal or a measure of health.

How to read your result

The large box names the shape or shapes that fit, followed by the waist-hip ratio. If there is a note about the ratio, it refers to the World Health Organization’s guidance. Try measuring twice on different days to check the result is stable.

Body shape categories are descriptive and are not a measure of health. This calculator is for outfit ideas, not medical advice.

Common questions

What are the female body shapes?

This calculator uses seven shapes: hourglass, bottom hourglass, top hourglass, spoon, triangle, inverted triangle and rectangle. Each is defined by how the bust, waist and hips compare.

Why does the calculator give two shapes?

The rules for the shapes overlap, so some measurements match more than one. In that case the calculator lists each shape that fits.

Why can the calculator not classify my shape?

The seven shapes do not cover every possible body. If your measurements fall outside all the rules, the calculator says so. It does not mean anything is wrong.

What is a healthy waist-hip ratio?

For women, a ratio of 0.85 or below is generally considered lower risk by the World Health Organization. Ratios above that point to more abdominal fat.

How is the high hip measured?

It is taken at the upper swell of the hip over the pelvis, around 7 inches or 18 centimeters below the natural waist.

Does body shape say anything about my health?

No. Shape describes proportions and is mainly useful for choosing clothes. Waist-hip ratio is one health screening measure but is not a diagnosis.
