Bits, bytes and bandwidth

A bit is the smallest unit of information, either 0 or 1. A byte is 8 bits. Data sizes of files are usually given in bytes, while network speeds are given in bits per second, which is why a 100 Mbit/s connection moves at most 12.5 megabytes each second. In this calculator kilo, mega, giga and tera follow the decimal system, so a kilobit is 1,000 bits and a megabyte is 1,000,000 bytes.

Download and upload time

The time is the size of the file in bits divided by the speed in bits per second. A 500 MB file is 4,000 megabits, and at 5 Mbit/s that takes 800 seconds, which is 13 minutes and 20 seconds. This is the best case. Real transfers are slower because of protocol overhead such as TCP and encryption, shared connections, and limits at the server end, so a stated bandwidth is not the rate you will always see.

Website bandwidth

Multiply the page views in a period by the average page size to find the data sent in that period, then spread it over the seconds in the period. The month used here is one twelfth of a 365.25 day year, which is 30.4375 days. The redundancy factor adds headroom for traffic spikes, since a link sized for the average would be overwhelmed at peak. Include the traffic from search engine bots, which can be higher than the traffic from real visitors.

Hosting plans

Hosts often sell a monthly data allowance, while network gear is rated in Mbit/s. The hosting converter changes one into the other by treating the allowance as spread evenly over the month. A 1,000 GB allowance, for example, is about 3.04 Mbit/s sustained. Real traffic comes in bursts, so the peak speed you need is higher than this average.

Real-world speeds are always lower than the stated bandwidth because of overhead and shared connections.

Common questions

How many megabytes per second is 100 Mbit/s?

Divide by 8. A speed of 100 megabits per second moves at most 12.5 megabytes per second.

How long does it take to download a 1 GB file?

At 100 Mbit/s it takes 80 seconds, which is 1 minute and 20 seconds, in the best case. At 10 Mbit/s it takes 800 seconds.

What is the difference between a megabit and a megabyte?

A megabyte is 8 megabits. Lowercase b is used for bits and uppercase B for bytes.

How much bandwidth does my website need?

Multiply the page views by the page size to find the data per period, then convert to Mbit/s and add a redundancy factor, such as 2, for peaks.

Are kilobytes 1,000 or 1,024 bytes?

This calculator uses 1,000, the decimal standard used for network speeds and many storage devices. Operating systems sometimes use 1,024 and call them kibibytes or just kilobytes.
