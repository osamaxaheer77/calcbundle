Time Card Calculator

Enter your work hours across the week and check any break, overtime or wage settings. This is a gross-pay estimate; payroll rules and overtime eligibility depend on employer and jurisdiction.

Quick answer. A time card totals the hours between clock-in and clock-out, less breaks. 8:30AM to 5:00PM with a 30 minute break is 8 hours, so five days make 40 hours, or $800.00 at $20 an hour. Overtime is usually paid at 1.5 times after 40 hours a week.

How the hours are worked out

For each day the calculator takes the time you finished and subtracts the time you started. A shift that ends earlier on the clock than it starts, such as 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM, counts as an overnight shift. Your unpaid break is then taken off, and if a break is longer than the shift the day counts as 0. The days are added up for the week. Times can be entered as 8:30AM, 8.30, 8:30 or 15:30; without AM or PM they are read as 24-hour time. A break is entered as 0:30 or just 30 (minutes).

Worked example with the default numbers

The page opens with Monday filled in: 8:30AM to 5:00PM with a 0:30 break. That is 8 hours 30 minutes minus 30 minutes, or 8 hours. Use the copy button to fill the other weekdays with the same shift and five 8-hour days make 40 hours. At $20 an hour the gross pay is $800.00 before deductions. An overnight shift of 10:00PM to 6:00AM with a 30 minute break is 7h 30m.

Overtime

For pay you can choose how overtime works: no overtime rate, overtime after a number of hours in a day, overtime after a number of hours in a week, or a cap on paid hours with no overtime. The overtime rate is a multiple of the base rate, commonly 1.5 times. Under the US Fair Labor Standards Act, covered employees are generally owed overtime at not less than one and a half times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek; some states add daily overtime rules.

A 45-hour week. Five 9-hour days (8:00AM to 5:30PM, 30 minute break) at $20 with overtime after 40 hours a week at 1.5 times: 40 hours at $20 is $800.00 and 5 overtime hours at $30 is $150.00, a total of $950.00. Daily overtime after 8 hours gives the same $950.00 here, because each day has one extra hour.

Daily versus weekly overtime

The two rules can give different answers. For an uneven week of 12, 6, 6, 6 and 6 hours (36 hours), a weekly rule pays no overtime ($720.00), but a rule that pays overtime after 8 hours a day counts 4 hours of overtime on the 12-hour day ($760.00). With no overtime rate at all, the week pays $720.00.

Rounding

Some employers round the time. With rounding on, the calculator rounds to the nearest 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, or the nearest hour. Daily overtime rules round each day, and weekly rules round the weekly total. For a shift of 8:07AM to 4:52PM with a 30 minute break, the worked time is 8h 15m, and rounding changes it like this:

Tips for an accurate time card

  • Record the real start and end times, not the scheduled ones.
  • Enter unpaid breaks only: paid breaks should stay in the working time.
  • Use AM and PM or 24-hour times consistently.
  • Check which days your employer counts as the workweek: it can start on any day.

What the calculator does not do

  • Taxes and deductions: the pay is gross.
  • Pay periods other than a week.
  • Special rules such as double time, holiday pay or on-call time.
  • Legal advice: overtime law differs by country, state and type of job.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting AM or PM, so 8:30 is read as 08:30 and 5:00 as 05:00.
  • Entering a break as 30:00 instead of 30 or 0:30.
  • Mixing daily and weekly overtime rules.
  • Counting a paid lunch as unpaid.

Common questions

How do I calculate hours worked from a time card?

Subtract the start time from the end time for each day, take off your unpaid breaks, and add up the days. This calculator does it for the whole week.

How is overtime calculated?

Hours above the limit you set, per day or per week, are paid at the overtime rate, which is the base rate times a multiplier such as 1.5. Hours up to the limit are paid at the base rate.

How do I enter an overnight shift?

Just enter the start and end times, for example 10:00PM and 6:00AM. When the end time is earlier than the start, the shift is treated as running past midnight.

How do I enter a 30 minute break?

Type 0:30 or 30. A number on its own is read as minutes, and a time with a colon is read as hours and minutes.

Is my time card saved or sent anywhere?

No. If you tick the save box, values are stored only in this browser on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Is overtime the same everywhere?

No. In the US, federal law generally requires overtime after 40 hours in a workweek for covered employees, and some states add daily overtime. Check your local rules and contract.
