Add or Subtract Two Time Values

Add or Subtract Time from a Date

By Osama · Last reviewed November 6, 2026

Quick answer. To add times, convert them to seconds, add, and convert back: 60 seconds make a minute, 60 minutes an hour and 24 hours a day. 2 days 5 hours 30 minutes plus 1 day 20 hours 45 minutes 20 seconds is 4 days, 2 hours, 15 minutes, 20 seconds.

How the calculator adds and subtracts time

Time is not decimal: there are 60 seconds in a minute, 60 minutes in an hour and 24 hours in a day. To avoid carrying mistakes the calculator converts everything to seconds, adds or subtracts, and then converts back into days, hours, minutes and seconds. The page has two tools: add or subtract two durations and add or subtract a duration from a date and time.

  • 1 minute = 60 seconds
  • 1 hour = 60 minutes = 3,600 seconds
  • 1 day = 24 hours = 86,400 seconds

Worked example: adding two durations

The calculator starts with 2 days 5 hours 30 minutes plus 1 day 20 hours 45 minutes 20 seconds. In seconds that is 192,600 + 161,120 = 353,720.

Worked example: adding to a date and time

The second tool starts from a date and time and adds a duration. Starting at 9:30 AM on January 15, 2026 and adding 3 days 18 hours 45 minutes gives January 19, 2026 at 4:15 AM, a Monday. The 18 hours 45 minutes carry the clock past midnight, which is why the result lands on January 19 rather than January 18.

Adding a list of times (laps, tracks, shifts)

To total several times, add them one after another. For example, 1:45:30 + 2:15:50 + 0:58:20 = 17,980 seconds, which is 4:59:40 (about 4.99 hours). Enter two at a time and use the result as the next input, or convert everything to seconds first.

Where people use it

  • Projects and timesheets: total the hours spent on tasks, then convert to decimal hours for billing.
  • Cooking and travel: add legs of a journey or stages of a recipe to find the total time.
  • Running and swimming: total lap or split times, or work out a finish time.
  • Media: add the lengths of videos, tracks or recordings to see the total playing time.
  • Deadlines: add a lead time to a start date and time to see when something is due.

Negative results

If you subtract a larger duration from a smaller one, the result is negative and shown with a minus sign in front of the days, hours, minutes and seconds. It simply means the second value was bigger. Use the Time Duration Calculator if you want the time between two clock times.

Daylight saving time and the date tool

The date tool adds an exact amount of elapsed time using your browser’s local time zone. Adding 24 hours is exactly 24 hours of elapsed time, so across a daylight saving change the clock on the wall can look an hour earlier or later than you expect. If you want the same clock time on a later date, add days with the Date Calculator instead. The tool does not convert between time zones; use the Time Zone Calculator for that.

Common mistakes

  • Treating minutes as decimals, such as 1.45 for 1 hour 45 minutes.
  • Forgetting to carry: 75 minutes is 1 hour 15 minutes.
  • Adding clock times (such as 3:30 PM plus 4:15 PM). Add durations, or use the Hours Calculator for shifts.
  • Entering days when you meant hours.
  • Expecting the tool to adjust for time zones.

How to read your result

The breakdown shows the answer as days, hours, minutes and seconds, and the totals underneath show the same amount as a single unit, which is handy for spreadsheets and invoices. For a date result, check the weekday shown to confirm the day is what you expected.

Common questions

Can I leave a field blank?

Yes. Any blank field is treated as zero.

Does this account for daylight saving time changes?

The date tool adds exact elapsed time in your browser's local time zone, so across a daylight saving change the wall clock can show a one-hour difference. To land on the same clock time on a later day, add days with the Date Calculator.

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60. 45 minutes is 0.75 hours, and 1 hour 45 minutes is 1.75 hours.

What does a negative result mean?

You subtracted a larger duration from a smaller one. The minus sign in front of the days, hours, minutes and seconds shows that the second value was bigger.

Can I add more than two durations?

Add two at a time and use the result as the first value for the next addition, or convert every duration to seconds and add them together.

Why is 90 minutes not 1.9 hours?

Hours have 60 minutes, not 100. 90 minutes is one hour and 30 minutes, which is 1.5 hours.
