Time Between Two Dates

Find the days, hours, minutes and seconds between a time on one date and a time on a later date.

What the calculator does

It finds the exact length of time between two clock times, down to the second. From 8:30:15 AM to 5:45:50 PM is 9 hours, 15 minutes and 35 seconds. The result is also given as a decimal number of hours, a total number of minutes and a total number of seconds, so you can use whichever fits your task, from payroll to timing a process.

Across midnight and across dates

In the first calculator, an end time that is earlier than the start time is taken to be on the next day, so 5:30 PM to 8:30 AM is 15 hours. The second calculator adds a date to each time, so you can measure spans of many days. It reports the whole days first, then the remaining hours, minutes and seconds, and also the total as hours, minutes and seconds.

Entering times

Type the hour, minute and second in the three boxes and choose AM or PM. An hour above 12 is read as 24-hour time. Use the Now button to fill in the current time from your device. The calculation uses clock times only, so it does not allow for daylight saving time changes between the two dates.

This calculator works from clock times and dates only. It does not know about time zones or daylight saving time changes.

Common questions

How do I find the time between two times?

Subtract the start time from the end time, borrowing 60 seconds from the minutes, and 60 minutes from the hours, as needed.

How many seconds are in an hour?

There are 3,600 seconds in an hour and 86,400 seconds in a day.

What if the end time is earlier than the start time?

In the first calculator, the start is assumed to be on the previous day. In the second, both dates are used, so enter the start before the end.

Does it include daylight saving time?

No. If the dates cross a clock change, the real elapsed time is an hour more or less than the clock times suggest.

How do I add time instead?

Use the Time Calculator, which adds and subtracts hours, minutes and seconds.
