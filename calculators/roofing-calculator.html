Roofing Calculator

Estimate roof area from a house's footprint and roof pitch, plus roof squares needed and an optional cost estimate.

By Osama · Last reviewed November 6, 2026

Quick answer. Roof area is the footprint times the slope factor √((rise ÷ 12)² + 1). A 2,000 sq ft footprint at 6/12 gives 2,236.1 sq ft, which is 22.4 squares, or 25 squares with a 10% buffer.

What roof pitch does to the area

Roof pitch is the rise over a 12-inch run. A 6/12 pitch rises 6 inches for every 12 inches horizontally. Because a pitched roof is longer than its flat footprint, the footprint is multiplied by a slope factor: √((rise ÷ 12)² + 1). A steeper pitch means more roofing material for the same footprint. The calculator takes the house’s base area, the pitch and an optional price per square foot, and gives the roof area, the roofing squares and the cost.

Worked example with the default numbers

The page opens on a footprint of 2,000 sq ft and a pitch of 6/12. The slope factor is √(0.5² + 1) = √1.25 = 1.1180. So the roof area is 2,000 × 1.1180 = 2,236.1 sq ft, which is 207.7 square metres or 248.5 square yards. In roofing, one square covers 100 sq ft, so that is 22.4 squares. With a 10% buffer for waste, cuts and overlaps it is 24.6, rounded up to 25 squares. Add a price per square foot and the tool also shows the estimated price.

Roofing squares and bundles

A roofing square is 100 square feet. Asphalt shingles are usually sold in bundles, and many types take three bundles to cover a square, though some take four or more, so check the pack. For the default 25 squares, three bundles per square would be 75 bundles. Ask your supplier for the coverage of the exact product.

Other units

You can enter the footprint in square feet, square metres or square yards. A footprint of 200 m² is 2,152.8 sq ft, which gives a 6/12 roof of 2,406.9 sq ft and 27 squares. A footprint of 222 square yards is 1,998 sq ft.

Estimating the cost

Enter a price per square foot of roof area to get an estimate. At $5 per square foot the default roof costs $11,180.34, and at $8 it costs $17,888.54. Real prices depend on the material, your area, the pitch and access, and often include removal of the old roof, underlayment, flashing and labour, so ask for quotes.

What the calculator leaves out

  • Overhangs. The footprint should include the eaves, which add to the roof area.
  • Complex shapes. Hips, valleys and dormers waste more material, so add 15% or more. For the default roof, a 15% allowance means 26 squares.
  • Exact bundles and rolls. These depend on the roof layout and the product.
  • Steepness and access. Steep roofs cost more to install because they need more safety equipment and time.

Measuring your footprint

Use the outside measurement of the walls and add the overhangs. For an L-shaped house, split it into rectangles and add their areas, and the Square Footage Calculator and Area Calculator can help. If you only know the roof dimensions, measure each section along the slope instead.

Common mistakes

  • Using the living area of the house instead of the footprint with overhangs.
  • Entering the pitch as an angle. The tool takes the rise per 12 inches.
  • Forgetting that the cost uses the roof area, not the number of squares.
  • Ignoring waste on complex roofs.
  • Treating the estimate as a quote.

How to read your result

The large number is the estimated roof area in square feet, followed by the same area in square metres and square yards, and the number of roofing squares with a 10% buffer. If you entered a price, the last line gives the estimated cost.

This is a planning estimate based on the footprint and pitch only. For an exact material list, measure each roof section and ask a roofing supplier or contractor.

Common questions

What is roof pitch?

It is the rise over a 12-inch run. A 6/12 pitch rises 6 inches for every 12 inches of horizontal distance.

How is roof area worked out from the footprint?

Multiply the footprint by the slope factor, the square root of (rise over 12) squared plus 1. For 6/12 the factor is 1.118, so 2,000 square feet becomes 2,236 square feet.

What is a roofing square?

A roofing square is 100 square feet. The default 2,236 square foot roof is 22.4 squares, or 25 squares with a 10 percent buffer.

Why add 10 percent?

The buffer allows for waste from cuts and overlaps. Complex roofs with hips, valleys and dormers waste more, so add 15 percent or more.

Does the footprint include the eaves?

It should. Measure the outside of the walls and add the overhangs, since they add to the roof area.

How many bundles of shingles do I need?

Many shingles take three bundles per square, though some take more. Check the coverage on the pack and ask your supplier.
