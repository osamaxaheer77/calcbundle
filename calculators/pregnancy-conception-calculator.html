Pregnancy Conception Calculator

Work backward from your due date, last period or ultrasound to estimate when conception most likely happened.

By Osama · Last reviewed November 6, 2026

Quick answer. Conception is about 266 days before the due date, or last period + cycle length − 14 days. A due date of Jun 15, 2027 points to Sep 22, 2026, with a most probable range of Sep 20, 2026 – Sep 24, 2026.

When did I conceive?

Conception happens when a sperm fertilises an egg, usually within a day of ovulation. Because ovulation is hard to pin down, the date is worked out from something you do know: your due date, the first day of your last period, or an ultrasound that gave the age of the pregnancy. The calculator shows a most probable range of about five days around its estimate, a wider range of possible dates, and the dates of intercourse that could have led to the pregnancy.

The date boxes open with dates relative to today, so the examples below use fixed dates. Type them in to reproduce the results.

From a due date

In a typical 28-day cycle, ovulation falls around day 14, so conception is about 14 days after the last period began and about 38 weeks, or 266 days, before the due date. For a due date of Jun 15, 2027, subtract 266 days to get a central estimate of Sep 22, 2026. The calculator then shows these ranges:

From the last period

If you start from your last period, the length of your usual cycle matters. Ovulation tends to happen about 14 days before the next period, so the estimate is last period + cycle length − 14 days. With a last period starting on January 1, 2026, a 28-day cycle puts conception on Jan 15, 2026. With a 35-day cycle it moves to Jan 22, 2026, and with a 24-day cycle to Jan 11, 2026.

From an ultrasound

For an ultrasound, enter the scan date and the length of the pregnancy it found. The calculator works back to the start of the last period and adds 14 days. A scan on March 1, 2026 that finds 8 weeks and 0 days puts the start of the last period on January 4, so conception is estimated on Jan 18, 2026. Early scans are the most accurate way to date a pregnancy.

Dates of intercourse

Sperm can live in the body for three to five days, so intercourse up to five days before conception can result in pregnancy. That is why the most probable dates of intercourse run from 5 days before to 2 days after the central date, and the possible dates from 8 days before to 7 days after. For the due date example, the most probable intercourse dates are Sep 17, 2026 – Sep 24, 2026.

What these dates cannot tell you

  • They cannot establish the exact day. Ovulation can come earlier or later than expected, and cycles vary.
  • They are not a medical or legal determination, for example for paternity.
  • A due date calculated from the last period may differ from one set by an early scan, and doctors usually use the scan.
  • Fertility treatments and irregular cycles shift the dates.

Common mistakes

  • Using the date of the last period as the date of conception. It is about two weeks earlier.
  • Entering the wrong cycle length. Count from the first day of one period to the first day of the next.
  • Treating the range as a single date.
  • Using a due date that is already an estimate from a different method.

How to read your result

The large range is the most probable conception dates, followed by the most probable dates of intercourse, the wider possible ranges and a reminder that they are estimates. Use the first as your best guess and the wider ranges to allow for uncertainty.

These dates are estimates only. They cannot establish when conception happened, and they are not a substitute for medical advice.

Common questions

How is the conception date estimated from a due date?

Conception is taken to be about 266 days, or 38 weeks, before the due date. The calculator shows a most probable range of about five days around that date.

How does cycle length change the estimate?

Ovulation happens about 14 days before the next period, so the estimate is the last period date plus the cycle length minus 14 days. A 35-day cycle puts conception about a week later than a 28-day cycle.

How long can sperm survive?

Sperm can live in the body for three to five days, so intercourse up to five days before conception can lead to pregnancy.

Why are there four ranges?

The most probable ranges are the best guess. The possible ranges are wider and allow for ovulation earlier or later than expected.

Can I use an ultrasound?

Yes. Enter the scan date and the length of the pregnancy found by the scan. Early scans are the most accurate way to date a pregnancy.

Is this accurate enough for a legal or medical purpose?

No. The dates are estimates only and cannot establish when conception happened.
