Mass Calculator

Mass is calculated by multiplying density by volume. Be careful to choose density and volume units that are compatible.

Quick answer. Mass is density times volume. 1 cubic metre of copper at 8,960 kg/m³ has a mass of 8,960 kg, which is 19,753.41869 lb or 44,800,000 carats. Use matching units, or let the tool convert the volume for you.

What the calculator does

For a uniform material, mass = density × volume. Choose a density and its unit, enter a volume and its unit, and the calculator converts the volume into the unit the density uses, multiplies, and shows the steps. The mass is then listed in nine other units, from milligrams to metric tons, pounds, ounces, carats, grains and atomic mass units.

Mass is not weight

Mass is the amount of matter in an object and stays the same everywhere. Weight is the force of gravity on that mass, and it changes with the pull of gravity. A scale that reads in kilograms or pounds is really measuring weight and converting it to mass for the place you are. A person with a mass of 70 kg weighs 686.5 N on Earth (70 × 9.80665), but only about 113.4 N on the Moon and 259.7 N on Mars. This calculator gives mass.

Units of density

Density is mass divided by volume, so its unit is a mass unit over a volume unit. In SI it is kilograms per cubic metre. Water is about 1,000 kg/m³, which is 1 g/cm³ or 1 kg/L. In US units, pounds per cubic foot and pounds per gallon are common. The list has dozens of choices, including every metric prefix per litre, ounces and grains per gallon, and tons per cubic yard. It also includes psi per 1,000 feet, a pressure gradient used for fluids in wells, which the tool converts to a density by dividing by the standard acceleration of gravity: 1 psi/1,000 ft is about 2.3067 kg/m³.

Examples in different units

  • A steel beam. 0.02 m³ of steel at 7,850 kg/m³ has a mass of 157 kg.
  • A concrete slab. A slab 10 ft by 10 ft and 4 inches thick has 33.33 cubic feet. At 2,400 kg/m³ its mass is about 2,265 kg. In pounds per cubic foot, concrete is about 150 lb/ft³, and 150 × 33.33 = 5,000 lb, the same mass in different units.
  • Water by the gallon. Water is about 8.345 lb per US gallon, so 55 gallons has a mass of about 459 lb.
  • A jeweller. One cubic centimetre of gold has a mass of 19.3 g, which is 96.5 carats.

Accuracy

The conversion factors use exact definitions: one pound is 0.45359237 kilograms, and one US gallon is 3.785411784 litres. Results are shown to about 10 significant digits. The density of a real material changes with temperature and with the exact material, so treat results for real objects as estimates.

Common mistakes

  • Using the wrong density unit. 8,960 g/cm³ is not the same as 8,960 kg/m³.
  • Mixing US and UK gallons. The tool lists both.
  • Confusing mass with weight.
  • Entering a density for one material and a volume for a mixture.
  • Assuming the density is the same at every temperature.

How to read your result

The large line is the mass in the mass unit of the density you chose, followed by the working: m = V × ρ with the numbers in. Below that are the nine other units. For the density or the volume instead, use the Density Calculator.

The result assumes the material is uniform. Hollow or mixed objects need a measured mass or an average density.

Common questions

How do you calculate mass from density and volume?

Multiply the density by the volume, using matching units: mass = density x volume. For 2 cubic metres of copper at 8,960 kg/m3, the mass is 17,920 kg.

What is the density of water?

About 1,000 kilograms per cubic metre, which is 1 gram per cubic centimetre or 1 kilogram per litre, at around 4 degrees Celsius. At room temperature it is very slightly lower.

What is the difference between mass and weight?

Mass is the amount of matter. Weight is the force of gravity on that matter, so it varies with location, while mass does not.

How many carats are in a kilogram?

There are 5,000 metric carats in a kilogram, since one carat is 0.2 gram.

What is an atomic mass unit?

It is one twelfth of the mass of a carbon-12 atom, about 1.6605 x 10^-27 kilogram. It is used to describe atoms and molecules.

Does it matter which density unit I pick?

No, as long as the number you enter matches the unit. The tool converts the volume to the unit the density uses and then multiplies.
