Conversion Calculator

Choose a measurement category, enter a value, and select the units to convert from and to. Temperature conversions require an offset as well as scaling, unlike ordinary length or mass conversions.

By Osama · Last reviewed November 6, 2026

Quick answer. A unit conversion multiplies a value by the size of the starting unit and divides by the size of the target unit. 5 miles is 26,400 feet, 8.0467 kilometres or 8,800 yards. Temperature needs a formula: 20°C is 68°F.

How unit conversion works

Every unit in a category is defined relative to one base unit: metres for length, square metres for area, litres for volume and kilograms for weight. To convert, the calculator multiplies the value by the size of the “From” unit in the base unit and divides by the size of the “To” unit: result = value × (from factor ÷ to factor). Temperature is the one exception, because the scales do not share a zero point.

Worked example with the default numbers

The page opens with 5 miles in the Length category, converted to feet. One mile is 1,609.344 metres and one foot is 0.3048 metres, so 5 × 1,609.344 ÷ 0.3048 = 26,400 feet. The same 5 miles is 8.0467 kilometres, 8,800 yards or 316,800 inches.

Exact definitions the calculator uses

The factors are the internationally defined values, not rounded approximations. Since the 1959 international agreement, the inch is exactly 2.54 centimetres and the pound exactly 0.45359237 kilograms, and the other US customary units follow from these. NIST publishes the same factors.

Temperature needs a formula

Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin start at different points, so multiplying by a fixed factor is wrong. The calculator converts through Celsius: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32, °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9 and K = °C + 273.15. For example, 20°C is 68°F, normal body temperature of 98.6°F is 37°C, and 0 K is −273.15°C. The two scales read the same at −40.

Look-alike units

  • US and imperial volume: a US gallon is 3.785 litres and an imperial gallon is 4.546 litres. This calculator uses US units.
  • Short and metric tons: a US (short) ton is 2,000 pounds, a metric ton is 1,000 kilograms (about 2,204.6 pounds).
  • Fluid and weight ounces: fluid ounces measure volume and ounces measure weight. They are not interchangeable.
  • Statute and nautical miles: this tool uses the statute mile (1,609.344 m).

Quick mental checks

A rough estimate helps you spot a typing mistake before you trust the result:

  • Miles to kilometres: multiply by 1.6. Five miles is about 8 km, close to the exact 8.0467.
  • Kilograms to pounds: multiply by 2.2. A 60 kg person is about 132 lb (exactly 132.2774 lb).
  • Litres to US gallons: divide by 3.8. Ten litres is about 2.6 gallons (exactly 2.6417).
  • Celsius to Fahrenheit: double it and add 30 for a rough figure. 20°C gives about 70°F, against the exact 68°F.

Rounding and display

The result is shown with up to six decimal places. Very large numbers (a billion or more) and very small ones (below one millionth) appear in scientific notation. Round the final answer to the precision you need, and convert in one step instead of chaining conversions, so rounding errors do not stack.

Common mistakes

  • Mixing categories, such as trying to turn miles into pounds.
  • Using a US recipe cup or gallon with imperial measurements.
  • Multiplying a temperature by a factor instead of using the formula.
  • Rounding in the middle of a multi-step conversion.

How to read your result

The big number is the converted value in the unit you chose, and the line below it repeats your input. If the answer looks wrong, check that the category and both units are right, and whether the unit is US or imperial. For circle measurements use the Circle Calculator; for material volumes see the Concrete Calculator.

Conversions use exact international definitions. For legal, trade or engineering work, follow the standard that applies to you.

Common questions

Why does switching categories reset the units?

Each category has its own compatible units. Length and weight, for example, cannot be converted into each other, so the From and To lists update to match the category you pick.

Can I convert between metric and imperial units?

Yes. Pick any From unit and any To unit within the same category, metric or US customary.

How accurate are the conversions?

The factors are the exact international definitions, such as 1 mile = 1,609.344 metres and 1 pound = 0.45359237 kilograms. The result is displayed to six decimal places.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5 and add 32. For example 20 degrees Celsius is 20 x 9/5 + 32 = 68 degrees Fahrenheit.

Is the gallon in this calculator US or imperial?

It is the US gallon (3.785 litres). The imperial gallon used in the UK is about 4.546 litres.

Why do very large or small results use scientific notation?

Numbers of a billion or more, or smaller than one millionth, are shown in scientific notation to keep the result readable.
