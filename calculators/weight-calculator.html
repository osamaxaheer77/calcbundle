How the conversion works

Every unit in the list is defined as a fixed number of kilograms. To convert, the calculator turns your value into kilograms and then divides by the size of the unit you want. For example, 1 pound is exactly 0.45359237 kilogram, so 2.5 pounds is 1.133980925 kilograms, and since there are 16 ounces in a pound, 2.5 pounds is 40 ounces. Results are shown to about 10 significant digits, and the box under the result lists your value in every unit.

To go from kilograms to pounds, multiply by 2.2046226; to go from pounds to kilograms, multiply by 0.45359237. A person of 70 kg weighs 154.3235835 lb, and a 150 lb person has a mass of 68.0388555 kg.

Stone, for body weight

In the UK and Ireland, body weight is often given in stones and pounds. A UK stone is 14 pounds, or 6.3503 kg. So 70 kg is 11.02311311 stone, which is 11 stone 0.3 pounds. The US stone in the list is a rarely used unit, half of a US quarter, or 12.5 pounds.

Units with more than one meaning

Several words mean different amounts in different places.

  • A US, or short, ton is 2,000 pounds, which is 907.18 kg.
  • A UK, or long, ton is 2,240 pounds, 1,016.05 kg.
  • A metric ton (tonne) is 1,000 kilograms.
  • The US quarter is 25 pounds and the UK quarter is 28 pounds.
  • The stone is 14 pounds in the UK and 12.5 pounds in the US.

A long ton is 1.12 short tons, and a metric ton is about 1.1023 short tons. Check which ton a price or a shipping document uses before you convert.

Gems and small masses

A carat, used for gems, is exactly 0.2 gram, so a 2-carat diamond has a mass of 0.4 g. A grain is 64.79891 milligrams, and 7,000 grains make a pound. The atomic mass unit, about 1.6605 × 10−27 kg, is used for atoms and molecules.

Weight and mass

In everyday speech, weight and mass are used for the same thing, and the units in this converter are all units of mass. Strictly, mass is the amount of matter and does not change with location, while weight is the force of gravity on that mass. The kilogram is the SI unit of mass. A scale that reads kilograms or pounds is measuring weight and converting it to mass for the place where you are. The Mass Calculator works out mass from density and volume.

Why different units exist

Early weights were set by whatever was easy to measure, such as a quantity of grain or the load a person could carry, and they varied from place to place. The pound and ounce come from Roman and medieval trade units. In 1959 the US, UK and other countries agreed the pound to be exactly 0.45359237 kilogram, which is why every unit in the calculator can be tied to the kilogram.

Common mistakes

  • Using the wrong ton. A short ton and a metric ton differ by about 10%.
  • Mixing the UK and US stone. The UK stone is 14 pounds.
  • Treating an ounce as a unit of volume. Here it is a unit of mass, not a fluid ounce.
  • Forgetting that gold and gems may use troy units, which are not in this list.
  • Rounding too early when converting in two steps.

Common questions

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

There are about 2.2046 pounds in a kilogram. Going the other way, one pound is exactly 0.45359237 kilograms.

How many ounces are in a pound?

There are 16 ounces in a pound, so 2.5 pounds is 40 ounces.

What is the difference between a short ton, a long ton and a metric ton?

A short (US) ton is 2,000 pounds, a long (UK) ton is 2,240 pounds, and a metric ton is 1,000 kilograms.

How many pounds are in a stone?

A UK stone is 14 pounds, about 6.35 kilograms. The US stone in the calculator is 12.5 pounds and is rarely used.

What is the difference between weight and mass?

Mass is the amount of matter, while weight is the force of gravity on it. Everyday weight converters, including this one, convert units of mass.

How many grams is a carat?

A carat, used for gems, is exactly 0.2 gram.
