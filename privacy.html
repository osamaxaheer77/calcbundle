Privacy Policy

Short version: what you type into our calculators stays in your browser. We do use Google Analytics to count visits, and we may show ads from Google, which use cookies, as described below.

Last updated: October 2026

What you type into the calculators

CalcBundle is a static site. Every calculator is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript that runs on your own device. The numbers you enter, the results and any settings you change are worked out in your browser and are not sent to us. We have no database, no accounts and no server-side code that receives or stores that information.

Information collected automatically

Like any website, the service that hosts these pages receives basic technical information when your browser asks for a page, such as your IP address, browser type, device type and which page was requested. This is handled by our hosting provider as a normal part of delivering the site.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit the site, which pages they read, roughly where visitors are located and which devices they use. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as your IP address, browser and pages viewed, and Google processes it for us. We use the results only to improve the site. You can learn more at how Google uses data when you use partner sites, and you can stop Google Analytics from collecting your data by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Exchange rates

The Currency Calculator and the forex calculators (such as the Pip, Lot Size and Margin calculators) load current exchange rates from a third-party service, ExchangeRate-API (with a backup service if that one is unavailable). When you open one of those pages, your browser contacts the service to download the rates, so the service can see your IP address and browser details, as any site you visit would. The amount and currencies you choose are not sent to it. The calculator may also save the last rates it received in your browser's local storage so it can still work if the service is unreachable.

The Mileage Calculator sends the two places you type to third-party services so that it can find them and work out the driving route: the Photon and Nominatim place searches and the OSRM routing service, all of which run on OpenStreetMap data. As you type in the From and To boxes, the text typed so far is sent to Photon to show suggestions, and when you press the button the places and route are sent to these services. They receive the text you entered, along with your IP address and browser details, as any site you visit would. The calculator may keep the places it found in your browser's session storage for the rest of your visit, to avoid searching again.

Advertising

CalcBundle is free, and we may pay for it by showing ads, including ads served by Google AdSense. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to CalcBundle and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see Google's advertising policies. The ads you see are chosen by those providers, and we do not give them anything you enter into a calculator.

Cookies

CalcBundle itself does not need cookies to run its calculators. The cookies on this site come from Google Analytics and, where ads are shown, from Google and its advertising partners. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The calculators will keep working, though some ads may be less relevant to you.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you may be asked for your consent before personalized ads or analytics cookies are used, and you can change your choice at any time.

If you ever notice this page doesn't match what the site actually does, please let us know via the Contact page.

Links to other sites

Some pages may link out to external resources or references. We aren't responsible for the content or privacy practices of any site we don't operate, and we'd encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party site you visit from here.

Children's privacy

CalcBundle isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will look into it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data, such as asking what is held about you or asking for it to be deleted. We do not keep personal data ourselves, since the calculators work in your browser, but Google and our host process the technical data described above under their own policies. You can limit that data at any time by blocking cookies, using the opt-out tools linked above, or using your browser's privacy settings.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, for example by adding accounts, saved calculations or new advertising or analytics services, we'll update this page and the date at the top so it stays accurate.
